# User-supplied artwork

These original PNGs were supplied in the chat. `rolling-hills-background.png` is the current first-scene background, with the earlier `meadow-background.png` preserved. The six sticker sheets supply the cat doodles, bunny characters, flowers, botanical borders, and smaller decorations throughout the introduction and arcade.

`heart-wallpaper.png` repeats as small tiles across the arcade wall, behind the existing decorations and checked floor.

`indian-dog.png` and `husky.png` are the supplied pet sprites. The Indian dog sits beside the otter and the husky beside the bunny; on smaller screens both pets sit between their owners.

The PNGs are unchanged. `css/personal-art.css` displays individual illustrations through CSS crop windows, with decorative layers excluded from screen readers and pointer input.
